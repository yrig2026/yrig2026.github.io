ABOUT YRIG

Turning disciplined saving into shared opportunity.

YRIG is a youth investment initiative designed to encourage members to save, invest and grow together. We aim to create a practical platform where young people can participate in financial growth, responsible investment and community development.

Head office: Mukono, Uganda.
